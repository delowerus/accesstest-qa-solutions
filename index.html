About AccessTest QA Solutions

AccessTest QA Solutions is an independent accessibility and software quality assurance service built on extensive hands-on experience testing enterprise and federal applications. Our founder brings Section 508 Trusted Tester expertise and practical experience with assistive technology, manual QA, and automation.

We focus on clear scope, reproducible issues, and recommendations developers can act on. A scoped assessment does not certify that a website meets all accessibility or legal requirements.
